# Regression summary (R)

Model: OLS on 2192 days, adjusted R² = 0.939, residual SE = 5.62 GWh/day.

## Key effects

- **Temperature (2025):** each heating degree day (1 °C colder on a heating day) adds **1.65 GWh/day** (95 % CI 1.55 to 1.75), about 1.0 % of an average winter day.
- **Heating sensitivity over time:** 1.33 GWh per HDD in 2020 vs 1.65 in 2025.
- **Cooling:** each cooling degree day adds 0.70 GWh/day.
- **Sunshine:** each extra hour of sunshine changes demand by -0.53 GWh/day.
- **Sunday vs Monday:** -25.8 GWh/day; public holiday: -20.2 GWh/day.
